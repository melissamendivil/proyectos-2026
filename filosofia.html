ABOUT OUR HISTORY

Founded in the scenic town of Pembroke, Massachusetts, Camp Wildfire was born out of a desire to keep authentic childhood adventure alive. We believe summer shouldn’t be remembered through a screen, but through the cool lake water on a warm afternoon, the smell of pine in the morning air, and the warmth of an evening campfire.

Embracing a classic retro-scout tradition, Camp Wildfire is a sanctuary from digital noise. Here, social media gives way to real-life social interactions. Through hands-on workshops, outdoor exploration in Pembroke's natural landscape, and a culture of mentorship, we empower campers to build confidence, master new skills, and forge friendships that last a lifetime.

One spark is all it takes to light up a summer. Welcome to Camp Wildfire.

"Getting kids away from screens and into real adventures is priceless."

OUR PHILOSOPHY

At Camp Wildfire, we believe that the most meaningful growth happens outside the comfort zone and far away from glowing screens. Our philosophy is rooted in the timeless traditions of classic scout camps: learning by doing, respecting the great outdoors, and fostering genuine human connection.
