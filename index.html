THERE ARE MANY SUMMER CAMPS, BUT THERE’S ONLY ONE CAMP WILDFIRE

Located on beautiful Lake Tispaquin in Middleboro, MA, one hour south of Boston, Wilfire boasts the ideal summer camp setting. Our cabins encircle our flat, well-manicured athletic field. A picture-perfect lake beckons behind the tall pine trees. The intimate layout means kids can traverse camp in minutes, allowing your son to get the lay of the land immediately. With only 150 campers, Avoda feels different from other camps. The oldest campers mentor the youngest campers, and everyone knows each other by name.

Come As You Are, Grow into your best self

Every camper comes to Wildfire at a different stage in his personal, emotional, and physical development. Your son will try new things, master new skills, and grow as a person — gaining confidence and building character.

TIME UNPLUGGED

At Wildfire, social media gives way to social interactions and meaningful connections. Texting, Snapchat, and TikTok are replaced by real-life activities that you can’t find anywhere else. Our environment encourages more traditional fun and making relationships in real life.
