WORK AT CAMP

Be Part of Our Team

WILDFIRE CREW

Behind every campfire song, lake adventure, and cabin laugh at Camp Wildfire is a dedicated team that makes summer possible. Our staff brings together counselors, activity specialists, kitchen and dining crew, and facilities and cleaning teams who care deeply about creating a safe, welcoming, and screen-free environment. Whether guiding campers through their first time on the water, keeping cabins and shared spaces tidy, or preparing hearty meals after a full day outdoors, every role matters.

Together, our team models kindness, responsibility, and teamwork so campers can step out of their comfort zone, build real friendships, and feel right at home from day one.

MENTORS & COUNSELORS

Cabin counselors live alongside campers, guide daily routines, and help turn everyday moments from cabin clean-up to late-night laughs into lasting confidence.

ACTIVITY LEADERS

From waterfront safety and archery to arts, crafts, and trail hikes, our specialists teach practical skills while keeping things fun, encouraging, and hands-on.

KITCHEN, DINING & CAMP CARE

Our kitchen and cleaning crew keeps camp running smoothly with hearty meals and clean, safe cabins and shared spaces every day.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Got questions about joining the Camp Wildfire team? Here are the essentials before you apply.

What are the minimum requirements?

Applicants must be at least 18 years old, pass a background check, and be available for the full summer session including staff training week.

Are room and meals included?

Yes! All summer staff members receive cabin housing, three daily meals in the dining hall, and scheduled days off during the session.

Do I need prior camp experience?

Not at all. While experience working with kids is a plus, we provide full hands-on training in safety, leadership, and activity instruction.
