OUR PROGRAM

Every day at Wildfire is action-packed from the first bugle at 8:00 AM to lights out at 9:30 PM!

No two days are ever the same, and campers are always on the move. We’ve designed a balanced program with something for everyone: sports, arts, waterfront adventures, day trips, and special events that create memories for a lifetime.

Grades 3-6

Arts (1x/week), instructional tennis and waterfront (both 2x/week) and THREE electives per session (each 3x/week)

Grades 7-8

Waterfront (2x/week) and FOUR electives per session (each 3x/week)

Grades 9-10

SIX electives per session (each 3x/week)

Campfire Storytelling & S’mores

Evening gatherings around the fire to share stories, sing songs, and make classic s'mores.

Trail Navigation & Compass

Learning to read paper maps and use a compass through the woods of Pembroke.

Lake Waterfront

Swimming, canoeing, and paddle adventures on calm waters surrounded by tall pine trees.

Arts & Merit Crafts

Hands-on workshops where campers create keepsakes, build skills, and earn classic camp badges.

Woodland Team Games

Classic outdoor games and group challenges that build confidence, teamwork, and real-life friendships.
